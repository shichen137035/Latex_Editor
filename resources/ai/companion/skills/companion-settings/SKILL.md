# Companion Settings

Search for the requested preference and inspect its real schema. Read effective values and inheritance before suggesting a change. Distinguish global and project scope. Questions about how to change a setting do not authorize a change. For requested writes, prepare a minimal before/after plan, follow the current approval policy, execute, and read back the result. Explain conflicts and offer undo.
